Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short: DocVault has no servers and no analytics. Your documents are encrypted on your device. If you connect Google Drive, only encrypted data is stored there, in a private folder that only DocVault can access. We cannot read your documents, and neither can Google.

Who we are

DocVault (“the app”, “we”) is a mobile application for storing personal documents. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. For questions, contact us through GitHub Issues.

Data stored on your device

Documents you add (photos, scans and PDFs) and their details (name, category, document type, expiry date, holder name, document number, issuer, country, notes and tags) are stored on your device.

  • Document files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, each with its own random key.
  • Those keys are protected by a vault key stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore on your device.
  • Decrypted copies exist only temporarily while you view or share a document, and are deleted afterwards.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Connecting a Google account is optional. You can use DocVault on your device only. If you sign in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

PermissionWhy
openid, email, profileTo show which account is connected (your name, email and profile picture) in the app. This information stays on your device.
drive.appdataTo store your encrypted backup in a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive. This permission does not let DocVault see, read or change any of your other Drive files.

The app stores the following in that folder: encrypted document files, encrypted document details, and a copy of your vault key that is itself encrypted with your recovery key. File names are random identifiers and reveal nothing about your documents.

Google sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device and are used only to communicate directly between your device and Google Drive.

Your recovery key

When you create a vault, the app shows you a recovery key once. We never receive or store it. It is the only way to restore your vault on a new device. If you lose it, nobody, including us, can decrypt your backup.

What we do not collect

  • We operate no servers. Your data is never sent to us.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • Document classification and text recognition (when available) run entirely on your device.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents, only when you choose to.
  • Photos and files: to import documents you select.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with Face ID or fingerprint. Biometric data never leaves your device’s secure hardware and is not accessible to the app.
  • Notifications: local reminders before documents expire. Reminders never include document numbers.

Sharing

A document is shared only when you tap Share and choose a destination, such as another app, AirDrop or Quick Share. That recipient’s handling of the document is governed by their own policies.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DocVault’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the backup and sync features described above. It is not used for advertising, is not transferred to third parties, and is not read by humans.

Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a document removes it from your device and from your Drive backup.
  • Settings → Sign out & remove from device erases all documents and keys from that device.
  • To delete your cloud backup, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → DocVault → “Delete hidden app data”.
  • You can revoke DocVault’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption and platform security features. No system is perfectly secure; keep your device updated, use a screen lock, and keep your recovery key safe.

Children

DocVault is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above.
